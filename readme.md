# 🌱 FarmIntel AI

**FarmIntel AI** is a smart, AI-powered farming assistant designed to support farmers with real-time insights, crop health analysis, and Tamil voice interaction — all in one simple dashboard.

> 🚜 *"Technology should not stop at cities."*

---

## 📌 Features

### 🔬 AI Crop Disease Detection

* Upload crop leaf images 📸
* AI detects diseases instantly
* Provides:

  * Disease name
  * Confidence level
  * Treatment suggestions

---

### ☁️ Weather Forecast & Alerts

* Real-time weather updates
* Rain predictions 🌧️
* Smart farming alerts (e.g., irrigation suggestions)

---

### 💰 Market Price Tracking

* Daily updated crop prices
* Tracks:

  * Tomato 🍅
  * Onion 🧅
  * Rice 🌾
  * Potato 🥔
  * Chili 🌶️
* Shows price trends 📈📉

---

### 💧 Water Management System

* Soil moisture monitoring
* Smart irrigation reminders
* Crop-based watering schedules

---

### 🎙️ Tamil Voice Assistant

* Ask farming questions in Tamil 🗣️
* Voice-based interaction
* Example queries:

  * “இன்று மழை வருமா?”
  * “தக்காளி விலை என்ன?”
  * “நீர் பாய்ச்ச வேண்டுமா?”

---
